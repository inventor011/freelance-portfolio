# YouTube thumbnail samples (1280×720)

> **Sample designs, not client work.** Made for this portfolio to show a cinematic, documentary-style look for Indian tech/finance channels. Background photos are AI-generated images; colour grading, layout, typography and text treatment are done in Python/Pillow by `make_thumbnails.py` (fonts: Anton, Poppins – Google Fonts, OFL).

Design choices: 3–4 word headline readable at mobile size, one accent colour per topic, subject on the right / text on the left so it never collides with YouTube's timestamp (bottom-right), dark left gradient for contrast, no misleading arrows or fake shock faces.
